    Our Craft

    Made Slowly, Made to Be Kept

    INSPIRED CREATIONS began with a simple belief: a gift should feel personal long after it is unwrapped. Every piece starts as carefully chosen natural wood or fine MDF board and is shaped, sanded and finished by hand in our workshop.

    No two pieces are ever quite the same. The grain, the warmth and the small marks of the maker are what turn an object into a keepsake — something to hand down, not throw away.
